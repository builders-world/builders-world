# Builders World asset direction — Yaba visual references

This is the technical art contract for the first local asset kit. The reference images are in `generated_images/chatgpt-yaba-visual-references/` and the extracted inspection crops are in `assets/reference/generated-visual-direction/extracted-crops/`.

## Visual system

- Place: Yaba, Lagos; dense, human-scale streets and shared community spaces.
- Time and atmosphere: blue hour through rain; cool blue-gray ambient fill; warm amber windows, lamps, stalls, and signs.
- Shape: chunky, readable architectural masses with softened bevels, simple roof forms, strong vertical street furniture, and broad silhouettes.
- Materials: painted plaster, weathered wood, brushed metal, clear glass, wet dark asphalt, concrete planters, broad-leaf foliage.
- Detail: concentrate detail around entrances, lamps, community boards, awnings, benches, signs, plants, and windows. Repeated background geometry stays cheap.
- Lighting: dynamic practical amber accents over cool environment fill. Do not bake shadows into base colors.
- Characters: provisional stylized builders with readable head/torso/leg masses, practical streetwear, backpack or tool accessory, and clear separation from the background. The final avatar is not approved.

## Runtime targets

- Three.js browser target; GLB/glTF 2.0; meters; Y-up; ground origin at `(0, 0, 0)`.
- Reuse material instances and geometry where possible. Keep repeated props under 12 draw materials per family.
- Avoid textures in this first procedural kit. If textures become necessary, use small shared atlases or KTX2-ready source maps rather than unique 2K maps.
- Each prop has a simple collision proxy when it blocks movement. Detailed render meshes never serve as default physics colliders.
- Provide a hero/near asset and a simplified distant variant where the object repeats across a district.

## Source and provenance

The visual references were generated by ChatGPT and supplied locally by Elvis on 9 October 2026. They guide form, palette, composition, and atmosphere. They are not directly repackaged as runtime textures. Production assets in this pass are authored procedurally in Blender; source scripts and the manifest record the relationship.
